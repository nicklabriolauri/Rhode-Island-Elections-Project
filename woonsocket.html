Woonsocket City Council

How at-large voting works

All seven councilors are elected at large. Voters across the city share the same council contest and can vote for up to seven candidates. Councilors serve two-year terms.

How seven at-large seats are elected

Woonsocket is one citywide ward. Every voter sees the same council contest and can mark up to seven different candidates. The seven candidates with the most votes earn seats. There is no fixed percentage threshold.

On this page, a candidate’s share is their votes divided by all council selections, including write-ins. One voter can contribute several selections. In 2026, Alana Gordon received 3,015 of 37,370 selections (8.07%), enough for seventh place. She finished 90 votes ahead of eighth place. The cutoff depends on the results of each election.
