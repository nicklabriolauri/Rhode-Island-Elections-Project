Comparing documented council sponsorship

Coverage and results

January 1 to November 9, 2026. One row per observed measure. We used direct lead attribution in the source, such as a named sponsor or an explicitly documented introducer. Cosponsors, movers, seconders, bare agenda names, introductions by request and known carryovers earn no lead credit in this test. The source records cover different sets of meetings.

For both cities, adoption credit requires the minutes to explicitly record adoption of the measure by the council. A referral, an amendment vote or a reading alone does not count. Signed texts and effective dates were outside this test.
