About Kristen E. Haroian

Kristen E. Haroian won a citywide Cranston council seat in 2026. She currently represents Ward 2 and serves as Council Vice President during the 2025–2026 term.

Haroian continues to serve Ward 2 during the current term. Her new citywide seat represents all six wards beginning January 4, 2027. Sara Jane Haupt won the incoming Ward 2 seat.

All Cranston voters, in Wards 1–6, elect the same three citywide council members, in addition to their own ward councilor. Voters could select up to three candidates in the 2026 citywide contest.
