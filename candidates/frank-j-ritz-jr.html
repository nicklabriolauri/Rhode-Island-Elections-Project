About Frank J. Ritz Jr.

Frank J. Ritz Jr. is Cranston’s current Ward 4 councilor and won reelection in 2026. His city biography describes a lifelong Cranston resident, former School Committee member and AAA Northeast operations leader.

Currently serving and reelected to a new term beginning January 4, 2027.

Cranston’s City Council has six ward members and three citywide members.
