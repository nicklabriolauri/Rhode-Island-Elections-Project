Campaign priorities

Local Control of Zoning

Opposes state-level zoning mandates that override local decision-making and supports preserving municipal authority over local land-use and zoning policy.

Electricity Costs and Climate Policy

Seeks to reduce electricity costs and opposes state climate and energy policies that he argues increase utility bills for Rhode Island residents and businesses.

Unfunded Mandates

Opposes unfunded state mandates that require municipalities or other local entities to implement policies without sufficient state funding.

Second Amendment Rights

Supports strong protections for Second Amendment rights, including constitutional carry, interstate reciprocity for firearm permits, and legal access to suppressors.

Agriculture and Rural Communities

Supports policies addressing the needs of farmers, agricultural businesses, and rural communities, with an emphasis on protecting Rhode Island agriculture and the rural economy.
