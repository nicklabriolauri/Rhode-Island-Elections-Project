Campaign priorities

Housing

Calls for a statewide rent freeze and expansion of affordable, publicly owned housing.

Healthcare

Supports healthcare for Rhode Islanders that is free at the point of service.

Power in the workplace

Supports a $30 minimum wage, freedom to unionize, and the right to strike.

Energy and infrastructure

Supports public ownership of Rhode Island Energy and full funding for RIPTA and public transportation.
