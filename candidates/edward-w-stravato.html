Campaign priorities

Small businesses

Supports removing red tape and incentives for businesses to stay, grow, and create jobs in Rhode Island.

Taxpayer oversight

First responders

Addiction

Calls for longer-term responses addressing the causes of addiction.

Local development

Supports local residents having a voice in what is built in their neighborhoods.

Support seniors
