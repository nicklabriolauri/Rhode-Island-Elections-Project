About this candidate

Samuel W Bell’s campaign positions and public records, with source and reporting-period details below.

At a glance

Samuel W Bell represents Senate District 5 in Providence. This candidate is listed as the Democratic incumbent in the 2026 candidate roster.

How to read this page

Campaign positions are attributed to the campaign. Election returns, finance figures and outside scorecards are labeled by source and period.

Campaign priorities

Health care

Supports restoring Medicaid funding, a Rhode Island Medicare for All system, and stronger protections against insurance denials.

Affordable housing

Supports zoning reform, more low-income and public housing, and greater use of federal housing funds.

Tax policy

Calls for reversing tax cuts for high earners to fund public programs.

Public education

Supports more school funding, building repairs, English learner support, and an elected Providence school committee.

Climate and energy

Supports expanded solar and wind power and opposes new fossil fuel infrastructure.
