How to read this analysis

Positions come from one-dimensional W-NOMINATE. Senators with similar positions tend to show similar voting patterns in this vote set. Party identifies the point color; it is not used to estimate positions.

The positive direction is anchored to Jessica de la Cruz. Zero is not a calibrated ideological center. Higher values do not mean better performance, and endpoints do not establish political extremism.

The combined fit uses 81 official journals, 366 divided roll calls and 50 bootstrap trials. Fifty-two conflicting source records are excluded. Ruggerio remains in the historical data but has only eight eligible divided votes, below the 20-vote minimum.

Annual fits are separately scaled. They should not be read as direct measures of ideological movement. Annual fits here have no bootstrap standard errors.
