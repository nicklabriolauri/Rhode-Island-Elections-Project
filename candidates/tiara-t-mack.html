Tiara T Mack represents Senate District 6 in Providence. This candidate is listed as the Democratic incumbent in the 2026 candidate roster.

How to read this page

Campaign positions are attributed to the campaign. Election returns, finance figures and outside scorecards are labeled by source and period.

Campaign priorities

Housing

Expand affordable housing, invest in public housing, limit rent increases, and strengthen tenant protections.

Education

Expand universal pre-K and public-college access while investing in teachers, smaller classes, and student supports.

Healthcare

Expand access to healthcare, including reproductive healthcare, and pursue universal coverage.

Criminal justice and gun policy

Pursue criminal-justice reform and stronger gun-safety policies.

Climate and wages

Advance clean-energy policy and support higher wages for working people.
