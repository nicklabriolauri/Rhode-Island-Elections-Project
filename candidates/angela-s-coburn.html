What the rating means & how it is assigned

The index reflects The Womxn Project’s assessment of candidate positions on reproductive rights and LGBTQ+ rights. Its staff and volunteers send a two-question survey, review public statements and social media, examine relevant voting records, and contact candidates about their findings. The tables do not specify whether an individual label is based on a survey response, public-source research or both.

For
Support for reproductive rights, gender-affirming care and LGBTQ+ rights under the organization’s definition.
Against
The organization finds opposition to policies supporting reproductive and LGBTQ+ rights.
Mixed
Conflicting information that the candidate has not clarified through the survey.
N/A
The organization could not obtain the information needed for a rating.

This is an outside categorical assessment, not an RIEP score or endorsement. RIEP does not combine it with legislative effectiveness or other scorecards, and does not infer a rating from party or incumbency.
