Campaign priorities

Public education

Fully fund Warwick schools, expand pre-K, and support teacher recruitment and retention.

Healthcare

Protect Medicaid, lower prescription costs, and expand mental-health services.

Housing

Expand affordable housing, speed permitting, protect renters, and assist first-time homebuyers.

Government accountability

Emphasize transparent, data-driven government and accountability for public spending.

Local economy and jobs

Support small-business lending, living wages, job training, and local employment.

Environment and climate

Protect Narragansett Bay, invest in clean energy, and strengthen coastal flood resilience.

Public safety

Support first responders, community policing, addiction recovery, and mental-health crisis response.

Seniors and veterans

Support senior services and veterans’ housing, mental-health care, and pension income tax relief.

Transportation and infrastructure

Seek road and bridge funding, improved public transit, and safer biking and walking.
