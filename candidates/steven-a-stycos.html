About Steven A. Stycos

Steven A. Stycos, known as Steve Stycos, won a citywide Cranston council seat in 2026. He has previously served on the City Council and School Committee.

Stycos is not listed among the city’s current 2025–2026 council members. His incoming citywide term begins January 4, 2027.

All Cranston voters, in Wards 1–6, elect the same three citywide council members, in addition to their own ward councilor. Voters could select up to three candidates in the 2026 citywide contest.

Background

His campaign biography describes ten years each on the City Council and School Committee, including four years chairing the council’s Finance Committee. It also describes work at Westbay Farm and community projects along the Pawtuxet River.
