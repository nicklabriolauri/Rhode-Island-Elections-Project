The Womxn Project defines bodily freedom as deciding what happens to one’s own body while respecting others’ bodily autonomy. The index focuses on reproductive rights and LGBTQ+ rights.

How The Womxn Project gathers information

Its staff and volunteers send candidates a two-question survey, review public statements and social media, examine relevant voting records, and contact candidates about their findings. Public-source research is used when a candidate’s position is not confirmed.
