About Avery Therese Mangahas

Avery Mangahas is the 2026 winner of Cranston’s Ward 1 council election. Her campaign describes her as a mental health counselor and founder of the Rhody Civics Club.

Councilor-elect; the new term begins January 4, 2027. Bridget R. Graziano is the current Ward 1 councilor during the 2025–2026 term.

Cranston’s City Council has six ward members and three citywide members.
