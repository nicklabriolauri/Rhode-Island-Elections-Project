About Daniel Robert Wall

Daniel Wall is Cranston’s current Ward 6 councilor and won reelection in 2026. The city lists him as Council President for the current 2025–2026 term.

Currently serving and reelected to a new term beginning January 4, 2027.

Cranston’s City Council has six ward members and three citywide members.
