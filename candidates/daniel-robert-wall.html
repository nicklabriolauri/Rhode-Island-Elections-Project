How sponsorship is counted

Each identified measure counts once per member, even when it appears at several meetings. Members explicitly listed together as sponsors receive joint sponsor credit; the first-listed name is not assumed to be the lead author. Explicit cosponsors are shown separately. Moving, seconding or voting for a motion does not establish sponsorship.

Recognition and commemorative items are separated from ordinances, policy-position resolutions and symbolic display policy. These categories describe the activity; they are not significance weights. Items first introduced in an earlier year can appear here when considered in 2026. Counts are not the number of bills introduced in 2026.

This pilot uses sponsorship clauses in the extracted records, plus a small set of checked cosponsor additions. Some source minutes contain sponsorships missing from those extracted fields. A missing entry does not mean a member did no work. No complete introduction denominator, passage rate, rank or overall effectiveness score is calculated.

Research background: Center for Effective Lawmaking. Its effectiveness framework also measures legislative progress and substantive importance; these municipal activity counts are not CEL scores.
