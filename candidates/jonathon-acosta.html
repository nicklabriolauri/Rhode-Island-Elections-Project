Campaign priorities

Criminal Justice Reform

Supports reforming solitary confinement and restrictive housing, including oversight of its use in Rhode Island correctional facilities.

Health Care

Supports protecting and expanding health-care access, including Medicaid, and opposing reductions that would restrict coverage or services.

Progressive Taxation

Supports a higher income-tax rate on high-income earners. The 2026 proposal would add a 3% rate on taxable income above $640,000; earlier versions used a lower inflation-adjusted threshold.

Housing

Supports expanding affordable housing and policies intended to increase the development and preservation of affordable homes.

Jobs and Wages

Supports policies aimed at increasing wages and improving economic conditions for working families.

Climate and Clean Energy

Supports policies to accelerate clean-energy development and address climate change.

Education

Supports increased public-education funding and additional support for high-need students, multilingual learners, and other special populations.
