Campaign priorities

Economic growth and reform

Roll back high utility rates; fight inflation and high gas and food prices; support income tax cuts for retirees and veterans; promote government accountability through a line-item veto and an Inspector General; attract businesses by reducing regulations, taxes and fees.

Enhance health care

Enable seniors to stay in their homes by eliminating the waiting list for home care and expanding Social Security tax exemptions. Use healthcare professional experience to advocate for vulnerable and elderly populations.
