About Sara Jane Haupt

Sara Jane Haupt is a civil and environmental engineer and the 2026 winner of Cranston’s Ward 2 City Council election. Her campaign describes an approach to city government informed by engineering, practical problem-solving, and working with people who bring different perspectives.

Office and election

Elected to the single-member Ward 2 seat in November 2026. Cranston’s council has six ward members and three citywide members.

Current representation

Haupt’s new term begins January 4, 2027. Kristen E. Haroian is the current Ward 2 councilor during the 2025–2026 council term.
