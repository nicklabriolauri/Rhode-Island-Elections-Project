What changed

A Democratic share increasing from 45% to 51% is a six-point swing. The Republican share falls from 55% to 49%, so the margin moves from R +10 to D +2: a twelve-point change. If you meant a six-to-eight-point change in the margin itself, the corresponding vote-share swing would be only three to four points.
