                                                                    Methodology & scenario comparison

                                                                    These preliminary ratings start with contested 2024 results and assume a 6–8 percentage-point increase in Democratic vote share. A one-point gain in vote share moves a two-party margin by two points. The midpoint is +7 points. This is a conditional scenario, not a polling estimate or calibrated win probability.

                                                                    Rating cutoffs use the leader’s margin: Toss Up under 2 points; Tilt 2–under 5; Lean 5–under 10; Likely 10–under 15; Solid 15 or more. Contested races within 30 points for either party in 2024 remain included; races entering that window under a swing scenario are added. Unopposed races and races without a comparable contested result receive no swing rating.

                                                                    House 15 holds Fung at an assumed 20%, drawing equally from both major parties. Senate 17 shifts the major-party baseline only; support for its independent candidates is unknown. Senate 19 is a Democratic-versus-independent race, so no automatic D–R swing adjustment is applied. Campaign finance and candidate characteristics remain context rather than separately estimated adjustments.
