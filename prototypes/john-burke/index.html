At a glance

John Burke represents Senate District 9 in West Warwick. He is listed as the Democratic incumbent in the 2026 candidate roster.

How to read this page

Campaign positions are attributed to his campaign. Election returns, finance figures and outside scorecards are labeled by source and period.
