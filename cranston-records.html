Meeting attendance

Coverage and method

Records are matched by individual identity and service period. The incoming term begins January 4, 2027. Sara Jane Haupt, Avery Therese Mangahas and Steven A. Stycos do not receive votes from the outgoing 2025–2026 council.

Named roll calls are extracted from the official minutes and checked against the reported tally. Unresolved names, ambiguous votes and source conflicts are kept in the review queue rather than assigned to members. A resolution's signature or sponsorship does not establish a vote.
