Sources & how to read these results

Vote totals come directly from the Rhode Island Board of Elections statewide general election feed. Shares divide each candidate’s votes by all votes reported for that contest, including write-ins. They may not sum to exactly 100% after rounding.

Reporting units are the state feed’s reporting jurisdictions, not a percentage of all ballots counted. Results may change as additional or corrected ballots are included. This page does not refresh automatically.

“Party change” compares the current leader’s party with the party that won the district in 2024; it is provisional until the result is certified. No independent race calls are made here. District boundaries are the same current legislative boundaries used in RIEP’s 2022–2024 maps. Surrounding-state boundaries come from U.S. Census data distributed by U.S. Atlas.
