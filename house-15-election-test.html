Evidence still missing

    Repository research records identify Paplauskas as the incumbent, Crudele as a Democratic challenger and Fung as an independent challenger with prior mayoral service. Those facts do not establish numerical vote bonuses. Endorsements, fundraising and primary votes are not converted directly into general-election votes.

    No individual voter turnout or precinct switching is modeled. This first version assumes a fixed electorate and allocates support between existing party pools. Added turnout, abstention and geographic differences require further data.
