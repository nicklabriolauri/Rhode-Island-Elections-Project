Mission Statement

Making Rhode Island elections easier to understand.

The Rhode Island Elections Project promotes a more transparent and accessible democratic process by providing open, nonpartisan access to election data, research, and interactive tools. Through maps, results, turnout analysis, and precinct-level information, the project helps Rhode Islanders better understand how elections shape representation across the state.

Public Goal

Build a clear, nonpartisan public resource that helps Rhode Islanders explore election results, turnout patterns, and legislative representation with confidence.

Project Scope

Cover Rhode Island election results, turnout maps, precinct tools, and district-level context from 2012 through 2024 in one accessible interface.

Map Color Guide

Across the 2026 filing map, blue marks Democratic-only filings, red marks Republican-only filings, teal marks Independent or Other-only filings, and purple marks districts where candidates from more than one party have filed.
