Local researchNovember 22, 2026 · 6:15 PM EST

New Cranston activity ranking and clearer council profiles

RIEP has added a provisional Legislative Activity Index for the nine members who served on Cranston’s City Council in 2026. Each named sponsor receives full credit: 1 point for symbolic and advocacy measures, 2 for routine property or administrative approvals, and 5 for substantive local policy, fiscal measures and ordinances. Additional explicit cosponsorships are listed separately.

Profiles for six members of the 2026 council now show their activity rank and points, with the comparison to the council average combined into one clear summary. Daniel Wall and Frank Ritz are tied for first with 29 points each—53.5% above the council average. Wall’s profile also includes a newly added portrait. Incoming members are labeled “Not applicable” for 2026 and do not inherit their predecessors’ records.

The ranking links to measure-by-measure source records, topic tags, scoring explanations and downloadable data. It covers 42 identified measures across 19 available full-council meeting records. This is a partial record of documented sponsorship activity, not a finding of overall legislative effectiveness. Visitors can inspect the evidence and explore alternative scoring assumptions.

Candidate coverageNovember 22, 2026 · 12:04 AM EST

The Womxn Project’s BFFI ratings added to Cranston council profiles

RIEP’s nine Cranston council profiles now display The Womxn Project’s 2026 Bodily Freedom Forever Index categories, with links to the publisher’s Cranston listing and information about the rating.

Green identifies “For,” red identifies “Against,” and gray identifies “N/A.” N/A means insufficient information, not opposition. These are outside issue ratings, displayed separately from organizational endorsements and RIEP’s own research.
