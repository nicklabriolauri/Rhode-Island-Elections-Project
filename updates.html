A new home for RIEP announcements and color-coded map choices

RIEP has launched this project-updates archive to make new coverage and website changes easier to follow. Each notice includes a publication date and time, a permanent link and links to the relevant tools.

Candidate coverageNovember 22, 2026 · 12:04 AM EST

The Womxn Project’s BFFI ratings added to Cranston council profiles

RIEP’s nine Cranston council profiles now display The Womxn Project’s 2026 Bodily Freedom Forever Index categories, with links to the publisher’s Cranston listing and information about the rating.

Green identifies “For,” red identifies “Against,” and gray identifies “N/A.” N/A means insufficient information, not opposition. These are outside issue ratings, displayed separately from organizational endorsements and RIEP’s own research.

Public recordsNovember 22, 2026 · 12:04 AM EST

Cranston council votes, attendance and election history are easier to explore

Available 2026 full-council minutes now support member-specific vote records and opening roll-call attendance percentages. The archive links back to source records and keeps unresolved cases visible.

Attendance percentages exclude missing minutes and unresolved opening records; they do not establish presence throughout a meeting. Incoming members do not inherit a predecessor’s record. Profile election histories also include 2026 returns and verified 2024 ward results for five individuals.

Local coverageNovember 22, 2026 · 12:04 AM EST

Profiles now cover Cranston’s six ward and three citywide council winners

RIEP’s municipal coverage includes all nine winners of Cranston’s 2026 City Council elections: six ward seats and three citywide seats. Profiles bring together sourced background information, available priorities, election returns and public records.

The Cranston map links local election returns with council profiles. Available campaign finance filings are linked from the relevant profiles. Current service is distinguished from the incoming council term beginning January 4, 2027.

MappingNovember 22, 2026 · 12:04 AM EST

Find Cranston and Providence ward maps from the homepage

The “See Rhode Island Election Maps” button now opens a directory of statewide and local maps. Visitors can choose 2026 legislative returns, historical statewide views, Cranston’s ward map or the Providence council mapping pilot.

Providence remains pilot coverage. Local maps and profiles will expand as boundaries, official records and election information are verified.
