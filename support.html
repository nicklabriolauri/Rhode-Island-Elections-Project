Where funding or partnership can help most.

The next stage of the project is not just visual polish. It is sustained upkeep: keeping the map current, improving mobile performance, expanding turnout history, validating precinct-level sources, and maintaining a credible public research interface.

Priority 1

  • Reliable hosting and domain stability
  • Routine site upkeep across future election cycles
  • Fast fixes when data sources or public links change

Priority 2

  • Historical turnout recovery for older election years
  • Further GIS and district-boundary validation
  • Cross-checking official sources for long-term accuracy

Priority 3

  • Better mobile experience and cleaner page parity
  • Faster map delivery and performance optimization
  • Expanded documentation for public and academic use
