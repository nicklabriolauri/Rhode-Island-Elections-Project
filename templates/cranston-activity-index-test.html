This measures observed sponsorship activity, not effectiveness, policy quality or service to constituents. Missing sponsorships can affect members differently. These values are not ready for use as public performance ratings.

SCORING ASSUMPTIONS

Choose what counts

The starting weights are 1, 2 and 5. These are RIEP test assumptions, not CEL categories or validated weights. All identified measures remain available in the audit, including those receiving zero points.

How the index works

Weighted activity = sum of each measure’s tier weight × the member’s role credit. Named sponsors receive 1 credit, or 1 divided by the number of named council sponsors when sharing credit. Explicit cosponsors receive the chosen cosponsor credit.

Index = 100 × weighted activity ÷ the nine-member cohort’s mean weighted activity. A value of 100 is the cohort average under the chosen settings; 200 means twice that average. It is not a percentage, a benchmark of good performance or a measure of effectiveness.

What this test can and cannot measure

Included

  • Distinct identified measures, counted once per member.
  • Explicit named sponsorship and cosponsorship.
  • Three scoring tiers, with detailed action types retained.
  • One primary topic and up to two secondary tags per measure; tags do not change points.
  • Source actions, including continuances and amendments.

Not yet measured

  • A complete inventory of substantive amendment authorship.
  • Oversight work and follow-through.
  • All budget contributions or committee work.
  • Implementation, policy quality or constituent outcomes.

These missing dimensions are not treated as measured zeros.

Coverage, classification and credit rules

The snapshot contains 42 identified measures across 19 available meeting records. It was built from extracted full-council records, not a complete transcription of proposed legislation. Some source minutes contain additional sponsorships. Generic titles, late cosponsor additions and excerpts crossing item boundaries limit attribution. The ordering of members can therefore reflect extraction gaps as well as activity.

Scoring tiers, action types and topic tags are provisional coding decisions, not legal judgments about validity or authority. The symbolic tier covers symbolic and advocacy activity; the administrative tier covers routine approvals; the substantive tier covers municipal policymaking. The weights do not measure policy quality or assume that a regulation is more important simply because of its topic. In particular, identifying a school-related ordinance as a proposed local rule does not establish that it was lawful or adopted. State-directed requests are advocacy. Property sale approval is separated from general rulemaking.

All named council sponsors share equal credit under the split option. No first-listed sponsor is assumed to be the lead author. This denominator counts named council sponsors in the dataset, not outside institutions or authors. Cosponsor credit is per member, not divided across cosponsors. A person cannot receive both sponsor and cosponsor credit for the same item.

Procedural movers, seconders, attendance and ordinary Yes votes receive no score here. Adoption does not multiply points: a motion marked passed may simply continue, refer or amend an item. Earlier-year measures are activity observed in 2026, not new 2026 introductions. No adjustment is made for seniority, committee leadership or expected performance.

Scope of council powers and research background

The charter distinguishes council legislation and oversight from executive administration. School funding and contract ratification are specific responsibilities; they do not establish general council control of educational policy. The model separates municipal action from advocacy directed at the state. A request for state library funding is therefore tagged Education and libraries but receives the symbolic/advocacy weight. A health-awareness commemoration receives a Health tag without being treated as a binding health regulation. These are RIEP classifications, not categories assigned by CEL.
