What the council can do

View the charter powers
FunctionAuthorityCouncil roleLimits
Municipal legislation§§ 1-100–1-102, 2-200–2-203, 2-306Adopts ordinances and resolutions on municipal matters. Resolutions may pass at introduction.Legislative authority is subject to the charter and state law. Ordinary ordinances cannot be acted on at introduction and generally require majority approval at each reading.
Mayoral review§§ 2-201–2-203May override a mayoral ordinance veto with six affirmative votes.Mayor may sign, veto within ten days, or permit an ordinance to become law by taking no action within that period. The mayor also has an appropriations line-item veto or reduction power.
Budget and capital spending§§ 2-301–2-305Acts on annual operating and capital budgets and may authorize supplemental appropriations within charter limits.Mayor’s receipt and surplus or deficit estimates cannot be altered by council. Capital budget amendments must conform to the capital improvement program.
Borrowing§§ 5-100–5-101May authorize tax anticipation borrowing for the general operating budget.Subject to state law and charter limits. The mayor and finance director sign city bonds and notes.
Licensing and compensation§§ 2-308–2-309Serves as the city licensing authority and fixes municipal salaries.Both powers are subject to charter exceptions, and the licensing role may also be assigned elsewhere by ordinance.
Audit and investigations§§ 2-310, 2-400–2-404Provides for audit and may authorize investigations and compel witnesses or records.Investigative procedures, witness rights, and applicable open-meeting requirements apply.
Appointments§§ 2-500–2-501, 3-201–3-202Elects the probate judge and approves many mayoral appointments to department head and board positions.Nomination and approval are separate acts. Charter exceptions apply, including the mayor’s role as public safety director.
Police and fire chief appointments§ 4-700; Question 9 (2026)Question 9 proposes mayoral appointments of both chiefs with majority council approval.Unofficial state snapshot shows 1,802 approve and 492 reject. Verify certification and effective date before describing the amendment as operative.
Tax assessment appeals§ 4-1101May cancel all or part of a tax to correct an assessment following findings from the Board of Appeals.The finance director implements refunds based on council action.
Executive administration§§ 1-102, 2-307, 4-100–4-103Can change certain department duties by ordinance within charter limits.The mayor and designated officers administer city operations. A council vote or resolution does not establish implementation.

Methods and sources

To compare with Cranston, count one measure across readings and amendments, credit sponsorship or co-sponsorship only with explicit evidence, and track introduction, advancement and enactment separately. A mover or seconder is a distinct role. Confirm sponsor fields with original dockets and attachments, and match final texts to the numbered clerk registers before using the same 1–2–5 weights for symbolic, administrative, and substantive measures for comparisons.

Coverage varies by document. This page publishes observed activity and early measure coding. A comparable effectiveness ranking needs a more complete introduced-measure register, explicit sponsorship and final-stage documentation for both cities.
