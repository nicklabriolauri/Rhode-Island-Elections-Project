Find My Precinct

Enter a Rhode Island street address to locate its 2024 voting precinct. The tool highlights the matched precinct on the map and reports precinct turnout, district assignments, and the associated House and Senate legislative results for that precinct.

Official RI Lookup
Start typing an address and the page will suggest Rhode Island matches. Selecting a city or town helps narrow results, but it is no longer required. Street-only matches are approximate, so adding a house number improves precision. For official polling place confirmation, use the Rhode Island Department of State lookup linked above.
