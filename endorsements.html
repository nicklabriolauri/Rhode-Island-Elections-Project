Explore verified endorsements of candidates for the Rhode Island House and Senate. Endorsements are grouped by endorsing organization, with links to the underlying source.

Coverage note
This is a developing dataset, not a comprehensive record of every endorsement in the 2026 Rhode Island elections. RIEP reviews publicly documented endorsements from organization websites and announcements, candidate materials, and reliable reporting. New endorsements are added as they are identified and verified. The absence of an endorsement from this page does not mean that a candidate has received no endorsements.

Methodology: Only explicit, publicly verifiable endorsement announcements are included. Campaign contributions, photographs, event appearances and other inferred forms of support are not treated as endorsements. RIEP does not infer an endorsement from party affiliation or other political activity. Source links open the material used to verify each entry. Collection is ongoing, so the database should not be interpreted as exhaustive. Where an organization uses a different designation such as “supported,” “recommended,” or another distinction, RIEP should preserve that organization's terminology rather than silently treating it as a formal endorsement.
