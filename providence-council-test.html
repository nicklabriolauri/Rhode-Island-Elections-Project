About this test

This section demonstrates local district mapping. Current councilors are shown as officeholders, not as a confirmed election candidate list. Candidate research, campaign finance and local effectiveness scores have not been added to this pilot.

Boundaries combine Providence precincts by the Ward field in Rhode Island’s public 2022 precinct dataset. Address matching uses the original precinct geometry. Confirm election-specific assignments with the official city ward maps or the Board of Canvassers.
